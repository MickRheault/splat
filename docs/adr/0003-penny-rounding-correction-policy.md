# Penny-Rounding Correction Policy

To guarantee the sum of rounded participant shares exactly matches the total calculated Bill amount, the system resolves any remaining fractional-cent rounding discrepancy (usually +/- $0.01 or $0.02) by adjusting the share of the Participant with the highest payable total. This ensures mathematical exactness and auditability, while minimizing the percentage impact of the rounding correction on any single individual.
