Privacy Policy

Splat: Receipt & Bill Splitter • Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Thank you for using Splat ("the App"). Your privacy and transparency regarding how your data is handled are fundamental to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Splat collects, how it is used, and the choices you have concerning your data.

1. Information We Collect

Splat is designed with privacy and zero friction in mind. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal credentials (such as your name, password, or credit card details) to use the App.

  • Camera & Receipt Photos: When you snap a receipt photo or upload an image from your device gallery, the photo is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our backend service solely to perform Artificial Intelligence Optical Character Recognition (AI OCR) to extract receipt items and prices. Receipt images are processed in memory and are NEVER stored permanently on cloud servers, nor are they used for advertising or model training.
  • Device Identifiers & IP Address: We collect an anonymous Device ID and IP address strictly to enforce free scan quotas, prevent automated abuse, and verify active in-app purchase subscriptions.
  • Local Bill & History Data: Your split bills, participant names, allocations, and saved receipt thumbnails are stored locally on your device's storage. They are not uploaded to central user databases.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To analyze receipt images and convert them into editable item lists.
  • To calculate itemized totals, adjustments, and tips accurately for participants.
  • To enforce free monthly scan limits and rate limits.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases (Trip Pass and Yearly Pro Subscriptions) via Google Play Billing and Apple App Store.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide core features, Splat integrates with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Google Gemini AI (Google Cloud): Used for secure, serverless receipt OCR text processing.
  • RevenueCat: Used to validate and manage in-app purchases and subscriptions securely without storing sensitive financial data.
  • Vercel & Upstash Redis: Used for secure cloud hosting, API serverless processing, and quota rate limiting.
  • Capgo: Used for over-the-air app updates.

4. Data Retention & Security

All data in transit is encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Receipt photos processed for OCR are discarded immediately after processing. Split history stored locally on your phone can be cleared at any time directly inside the App.

5. User Data Deletion & Rights

Because Splat stores your bill history locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all split history and cached receipt images by tapping "Reset All" or clearing app storage in your device settings.

If you have any questions or wish to request data deletion regarding your device identifier, you may contact us at the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Splat is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
